About me

I am P.M. Nithi, M.A., an astrologer with over 40 years of experience, dedicated to guiding people through traditional astrological knowledge. Over the years, I have had the privilege of serving millions of happy clients, helping them find clarity, confidence and direction in various aspects of life.

I proudly carry forward the legacy of my late father, Pauldurai Nadar, a renowned astrologer from Sithankudierupu, Thoothukudi, with over 70 years of experience. From an early age, I had the opportunity to learn directly from him, gaining in-depth knowledge of astrology, ranging from its fundamental principles and traditional methodologies to advanced calculations, planetary interpretations, and predictive techniques. His teachings continue to inspire my journey in preserving our family’s rich astrological tradition.
